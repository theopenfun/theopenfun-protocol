Built to be seen through.

Three habits shape every pixel here.

01

Fresh by default

Rows refresh on their own while you watch and rest while you look away. Nothing here needs a reload to stay honest.

02

One address, one truth

The contract bar at the top is the single source for our token. If it is empty, nothing has been announced. Trust it over any screenshot.

03

Quiet when unsure

A missing figure shows a dash. A failed source keeps the last good numbers on screen instead of guessing.

Plain answers.

Which address is real?

Only the one printed on the bar at the very top, once it appears. Anything else pasted elsewhere should be ignored.

Where do the numbers come from?

A public Solana market feed. Rows list the most boosted coins on the network, nothing hand picked.

Why is a row showing dashes?

The feed had no figure for it, or could not be reached. The board keeps the last good values and retries quietly.

Is this advice?

No. It is a window, not a recommendation. Coins swing hard and can go to zero.
