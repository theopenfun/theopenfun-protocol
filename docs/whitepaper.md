# The Open — Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Coins on Solana appear faster than anyone can read them. The Open exists to make that stream legible: one bright page where a newcomer can see what is moving, how deep the liquidity runs, and which address is the real one, without needing to trust a screenshot.

**Mission:** keep the market in plain view, say only what has been confirmed, and make launching a coin as light as looking at one.

## Principles

1. **One record per fact.** Every external fact (contract address, social links, data sources) lives in a single record that is either stated, absent, or unconfirmed. Anything not stated is shown as inert.
2. **The address bar is the truth.** The contract address shown at the top of the site is the only address that counts.
3. **Fail quiet.** When a source is unreachable the board keeps its last good values and retries with growing delays.

## Roadmap

1. **Phase 1 — Open the window.** Live board, ticker and contract bar for everyone.
2. **Phase 2 — Announce the token.** State the contract address once; the board adopts it and pins the token at row one.
3. **Phase 3 — Widen the lens.** Per-coin pages, watchlists and side-by-side comparison.
4. **Phase 4 — Hand over the keys.** Creator-side launch flow and community-steered priorities.
